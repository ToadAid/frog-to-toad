# Witness Review Protocol

How a Witness review is conducted under [witness-contract-v2.md](witness-contract-v2.md).

## When to convene

- Doctrine publication — a claim is about to become doctrine.
- Conformance promotion — a lane is about to be promoted on claimed evidence.
- A principal-requested independent review.

## Calling the review

- One call carries: the question under review, the atomic claims (each with a stable id), the subject paths, and the evidence paths.
- Claims are atomic: one assertable statement each. If a claim needs "and", it is two claims.
- Subjects and evidence are paths. No narrative brief, no summary of the expected conclusion.

## During the review

- The Witness inspects evidence by direct byte reads (contract term 4).
- The Witness report is recorded verbatim, unedited, before any reconciliation (term 6).

## After the report

- Reconciliation is authored separately. Every objection receives exactly one disposition (term 7).
- The principal's ruling is stated separately from the reconciliation.
- Unresolved items stay unresolved until new evidence exists. A review does not manufacture closure.

## Boundaries

- The Witness never approves, publishes, executes, or closes missing proof.
- Witness self-confinement claims go to principal/CI review (term 8).
- Agreement is evidence about the review, not proof of the claim.
